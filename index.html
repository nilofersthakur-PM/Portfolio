SMS-Magic

Onboarding Automation

Focus: Customer experience, workflow automation, time-to-value

The problem

Customer onboarding was highly manual and could take weeks before customers could start using the product. The process also created dependency on Customer Success for setup activities.

My role

As Product Owner, I worked with Customer Success, Engineering and other stakeholders to understand the existing journey, identify bottlenecks, define requirements and drive the move toward a more automated onboarding experience.

What I worked on

  • Mapped the existing onboarding journey and identified friction points.
  • Translated customer and business needs into product requirements.
  • Collaborated with engineering and customer-facing teams throughout delivery.
  • Focused on reducing manual intervention and improving time-to-value.
Impact
Onboarding time was reduced from weeks to minutes, significantly reducing manual effort and helping customers reach product value much faster.
A strong product experience often comes from removing friction from an existing journey rather than simply adding more functionality.
SMS-Magic

Campaign Creation UX

Focus: Usability, workflow design, product experience

The problem

The campaign creation experience could be made more intuitive so users could move more easily from configuring a campaign to generating and sending it.

My role

I contributed to improving the campaign creation experience by working with stakeholders and product, design and engineering teams to understand the existing workflow and define improvements.

What I worked on

  • Reviewed the existing campaign creation workflow and user experience.
  • Identified areas where the interface could be simplified.
  • Defined requirements for a clearer and more intuitive experience.
  • Worked cross-functionally to translate user needs into product improvements.
Impact
The work resulted in a more intuitive campaign creation experience, helping users move more easily through campaign configuration and execution.
Simplifying the path to an existing user outcome can create meaningful product value without adding complexity.
QAD

.NET UI → Adaptive UX

Focus: SaaS ERP, manufacturing, UX modernisation, release delivery

The problem

QAD's SaaS ERP platform supports manufacturers across manufacturing processes, internal collaboration, order processing, delivery and operational resources. A major product transformation involved moving functionality from the existing .NET-based UI to Adaptive UX.

My role

As Product Owner, I contributed to prioritising and moving product capabilities from the existing UI towards Adaptive UX and worked with cross-functional teams to support delivery through releases.

What I worked on

  • Understood existing functionality and customer workflows.
  • Prioritised capabilities for migration to Adaptive UX.
  • Worked with engineering and UX teams to define requirements.
  • Supported feature delivery across releases.
  • Identified dependencies and customer-transition challenges.
  • Contributed to workflow automation initiatives aimed at improving order creation and processing.
Impact
Contributed to the ongoing modernisation of the product experience and delivery of Adaptive UX capabilities through successive releases while balancing customer-transition considerations.
Product transformation is not simply replacing old technology with new technology; it is delivering a better experience while protecting customer workflows and continuing to deliver business value.

Approach

01 — Start with the problem

02 — Map the journey
